Portfolio Floor Value
The cheapest currently-open marketplace listing for every highlight/pack you hold, summed as-is — a snapshot refreshed hourly, not a live quote.

Not priced — an asset with zero open listings right now has no floor to sum; it's counted at $0 rather than dropped, and called out separately below the total.
Set Value
Based on the current floor price (the cheapest open marketplace listing) of each highlight in the set, summed — a snapshot refreshed hourly, not a live quote.

Set Value — cost to buy one of every highlight in the set from scratch, whether you own it or not.
Cost to Complete — same idea, but only for the highlights you're still missing. Blank once you own the full set.

A yellow (-N HL) next to either number means N of those highlights currently have no open listing, so they're left out of that total.
Rating Formula
(total assets − sum of editions + edition size) ÷ total assets × 100

Total assets — non-burned, non-system-held editions across every highlight in this collection (its real circulating supply).
Sum of editions — sum of your lowest-owned edition number for each highlight in this complete set.
Edition size — number of highlights that make up one complete set.

Lower editions = higher rating. Max is 100 (every highlight at edition #1) — it can go negative on a bad pull in a large set.
