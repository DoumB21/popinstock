Rating Formula
(total assets − sum of lowest mints + set size) ÷ total assets × 100

Total assets — number of non-burned NFTs across all holders for that set's rarities.
Sum of lowest mints — sum of your best mint number for each card in the set.
Set size — number of cards in the set.

Lower mints = higher rating. Max is 100 (every card at mint #1).
