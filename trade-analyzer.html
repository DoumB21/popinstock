⚡ Quick Start Guide

  1. Pick a Mode — Generic searches any WAX collection; Funko and Topps use their curated catalogs.
  2. Connect — Link your wallet via the button at the top of the site.
  3. Add Partners — Type target wallet addresses below, or open the blue TRADERS tab on the right to select partners.
  4. Build Offer — Search a drop, then click any Mint Number in the results table to add / remove it. Selected items turn green!
  5. Finalize — Close the Traders tab and click the orange TRADE tab on the right to review your active trade summary and send the offer.
